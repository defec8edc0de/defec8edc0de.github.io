Most Kubernetes trainings show you how to run a cluster. In **Offensive K8s Security 101** you learn how clusters fall over. You start by auditing a shared cluster the way an assessor would, then you attack it, and at the end everyone defends their own namespace against everyone else. I give it together with my ERNW colleague Sven Nobis.
